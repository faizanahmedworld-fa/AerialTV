How it works

Aerial TV is a small player. The channels come from free public streams that the open iptv-org project keeps lists of. We don't host any video ourselves.

Install Aerial TV on your device

Tap your device for the steps. The download link to type on a TV is https://github.com/faizanahmedworld-fa/AerialTV/releases/latest/download/AerialTVAndroid.apk

Amazon Fire TV Stick
  1. On the home screen, search for Downloader and install it. It's free.
  2. Go to Settings, My Fire TV, Developer options, Install unknown apps, and switch on Downloader. If you can't see Developer options, open About and click your device name 7 times.
  3. Open Downloader, type the link above and press Go.
  4. Press Install, then Open. Pick your country and press OK.
Google TV and Android TV (Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Sharp, Chromecast, Mi Box)
  1. Open the Play Store on the TV and install Downloader by AFTVnews. It's free.
  2. Allow Downloader to install apps. On Android TV that's Settings, Device Preferences, Security and restrictions, Unknown sources. On Google TV, click Android TV OS build 7 times in Settings, System, About first, then find Unknown sources under Apps.
  3. Open Downloader, type the link above and press Go.
  4. Press Install, then Open. Aerial TV gets its own tile on the home screen.
Android phone or tablet
  1. Tap the download button at the top of this page.
  2. Open the file when it finishes. If your phone asks, allow Chrome to install apps, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
iPhone, iPad and Apple TV

Apple only allows apps from its own App Store, and the App Store doesn't take players that come with channels built in. So Aerial TV runs as a web page in Safari instead. Open the browser version, pick your country, tap the picture once to start and swipe up or down to change channel.

For an Apple TV, tap the AirPlay button at the top and choose your TV. To keep it like an app, tap Share in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen. Only channels that play in a browser are listed, so the list is shorter than in the Android app.

Windows 10 and 11
  1. Download the Windows installer.
  2. Open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.
  3. Pick your country. Up and down change channel, Enter opens the list and F is full screen.
Samsung and LG smart TVs

Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS) TVs can't install Android apps. The usual fix is a Fire TV Stick or a Google TV box in the HDMI port, which costs about the same as one month of a paid TV plan. Then follow the steps for that device. A TV with a Google TV or Android TV badge, which includes all Sony sets and some Hisense and Philips ones, is Android inside, so the Google TV steps work as they are.

Full step by step guide with pictures: the README on GitHub.

What it does

The app is built to be used from a sofa with a remote. Up and down change channel. Number keys jump straight to a channel. OK opens the channel list, and holding OK on a channel adds it to your favourites.

Free streams go down all the time, so Aerial TV works around that. If a channel is off air it moves to the next one by itself after a few seconds. Channels with backup links try each one before giving up. The last channel you watched comes back the next time you open the app, and an update installed over the old version keeps your favourites.

On a phone, swipe up or down to change channel and tap for the list. On a Windows PC it does the same with the arrow keys and F for full screen, and the Windows version checks for a newer release each time it opens.

Questions

Is Aerial TV really free?

Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no ads inside the app.

Where do the channels come from?

From free public streams listed by the open iptv-org project. Aerial TV is only the player. It doesn't host, store or restream any video.

A channel says "off air right now". Is the app broken?

No. That free stream is down today, which happens often with free streams. Aerial TV jumps to the next channel by itself, so wait a few seconds.

A channel plays at home but not when I travel.

Some broadcasters only allow viewers inside their own country. A Pakistani channel might play in Lahore and stay dark in Dubai. That's the broadcaster's rule, not a fault in the app.

Why isn't it in the Google Play Store or the App Store?

Store review rules make a player with channels built in hard to publish, and Apple's App Store doesn't take them at all. So you install Aerial TV straight from the download link, and on iPhone it runs in Safari.

Is this the same as the older Aerial TV app for RTL-SDR?

No. That is a different project, for watching DVB-T broadcasts with an RTL-SDR receiver. This Aerial TV is a player for free internet TV streams and has nothing to do with it.

Can I see the code?

Yes, it's all on GitHub. If something doesn't work, open an issue.

Aerial TV doesn't host or control any of the streams it plays. They are free public streams published by third parties and listed by the open iptv-org project. If you own a stream and want it taken down, please tell iptv-org, or open an issue here and we'll look at it.
