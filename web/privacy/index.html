Aerial TV

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026. Applies to Aerial TV for Android, Android TV, Fire TV, Windows and the web player.

Aerial TV has no accounts, no sign in, no analytics and no advertising network. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server of ours to send it to.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things so it works like a normal TV. All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

  • The country, "who's watching", interests and languages you picked at first run.
  • The menu language, if you changed it in Settings.
  • Your favourite channels and the last channel you watched.
  • A small watch history (which channels you left on and for how long) that builds the Most watched row. It is capped at 60 channels per country.
  • How quickly each channel's stream answered, which draws the signal bars.
  • A cached copy of the channel list so the app starts offline.

You can wipe favourites and history any time from Settings, Clear favourites and history.

What we never do

  • No account, email, phone number or name is asked for or stored.
  • No location access. The country suggestion comes from your device's time zone and language settings, read on the device only.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs. No cookies in the apps.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.
  • No video is hosted by us. Aerial TV is a player for streams that broadcasters already publish openly.

Permissions

The Android app asks for internet access and network state only. It needs no storage, camera, microphone, contacts or location permission.

Children

The app is not aimed at children, though the Kids profile puts cartoon and learning channels first. We collect no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting anything, this page will say so first and the app version notes will point to it. Until then, this policy holds.
